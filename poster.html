Europe · Security
Rutte vows NATO will defend 'every inch' of Poland
NATO chief Mark Rutte visited Warsaw on Saturday and promised the alliance would defend every inch of Polish territory, a day after two Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down in the south-east. No one was hurt.

Middle East · Bahrain
Iran hits US Patriot radars in Bahrain; fire at civilian site
Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they fired missiles and drones at two US Patriot radars at al-Riffa and Awali. Bahrain reported a civilian industrial facility caught fire; no one was hurt.

Trade · Europe
EU and Indonesia sign landmark trade deal
The EU and Indonesia signed a trade pact in Brussels after a decade of talks, scrapping tariffs on up to 98.5% of tariff lines. It still needs approval by the European Parliament.

Climate · COP31
COP31 deadlocked as draft drops fossil fuels
Climate talks in Antalya ran past their Friday deadline with no deal. A draft dropped all mention of fossil fuels and a $120bn adaptation-finance target; a revised text was tabled early Sunday.

Mexico · Cartels
'El Chapo Isidro' captured; 11 dead in Sinaloa
Mexican forces captured cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, one of FBI's 10 most wanted, in Guasave. Gunmen set trucks ablaze and 11 people were killed in the fighting.

Russian strike kills two in Lviv
Two people were killed and ten injured when Russian missiles and drones hit Lviv's Sknyliv railway station in a huge overnight barrage of over 400 drones and 110 missiles.
